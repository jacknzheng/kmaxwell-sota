Next steps toward principled accumulation

Practical second-order optimizers already show that factored curvature estimates can improve training. The unresolved problem is how long to accumulate those estimates, how strongly to trust them, and how their accumulation should interact with momentum. Today these choices are tuned. The program below tries to derive them from measured noise and drift.

The decision

Can we replace tuned decay rates in momentum and factored curvature estimation with accumulation windows and shrinkage strengths determined from the training state?

What tonight changed

The useful momentum effect is concentrated in noisy, short-timescale gradient history. In the measured NanoGPT comparison, bi-Maxwell's advantage over no momentum decreases as batch size rises and reaches measured zero at \(16\times\) batch. A single exponential moving average adds no measurable benefit at either tested batch size. Kernel shape, rather than generic averaging, carries the observed gain.

Our full-Hessian measurements cannot yet derive a useful Newton operator. Heavily damped conjugate-gradient solves agree across disjoint minibatches, but their low cost and their shallow spectral reach are the same fact: the damping suppresses the poorly estimated spectrum. Individual eigendirections replicate reliably only near the top of the spectrum. Claims that the full inverse Hessian is cheaply available, that per-rank reliability determines a fractional Newton exponent, and that momentum cannot represent Newton have therefore been withdrawn.

The hindsight-action objective is no longer part of the program. It required future gradients and Hessians to choose a present update, yet it offered no convincing route to a deployable rule. Jeffrey vetoed that target. The dense recordings remain useful because they let us measure the noise and drift that an online accumulator actually sees.

A candidate: let replicas set both accumulation clocks

Every training step already produces eight independent microbatch gradients before data-parallel averaging. Their disagreement estimates sampling noise at the current state. Changes across nearby steps estimate drift. Those measurements can set the decay rate for both momentum and factored curvature accumulation.

The rule must be derived for the accumulator that will run during training. Let a geometric accumulator assign lag \(k\) the normalized weight

\[ w_k=(1-\beta)\beta^k, \qquad A=\sum_{k\geq0}kw_k=\frac{\beta}{1-\beta}, \]

where \(A\) is the average lag in training steps. If independent sampling noise has squared magnitude \(\sigma_t^2\), geometric averaging leaves \(\sigma_t^2/(2A+1)\). If the target moves locally at rate \(d_t\), stale observations contribute approximately \(d_t^2A^2\). The predicted average lag is therefore the nonnegative solution of

\[ A_t^*=\underset{A\geq0}{\operatorname{argmin}} \left\{\frac{\sigma_t^2}{2A+1}+d_t^2A^2\right\}, \qquad A_t^*(2A_t^*+1)^2=\frac{\sigma_t^2}{d_t^2}. \]

The drift fit must subtract sampling noise before solving this equation. For several fixed lags \(\ell\), the local model predicts

\[ \mathbb E\lVert X_t-X_{t-\ell}\rVert^2 \approx 2\sigma_t^2+d_t^2\ell^2. \]

The gradient calculation measures error after removing a common scale, because Muon's matrix sign discards that scale. The factor calculation measures held-out preconditioner-action error. A single average lag is fitted per matrix first; frequency-dependent clocks are considered only if scalar residuals exhibit stable spectral structure across training states.

Lead falsifier

With the norm, lags, token scaling, and geometric weights fixed in advance, the gradient rule must reproduce the observed shortening of the intern's eight-buffer K-Maxwell kernel (K8). Its average lag, computed from the normalized kernel weights, is approximately 49, 36, and 29 steps at the three saved training states. A fitted multiplier is forbidden. Failure rejects replica-clocked momentum. Success explains the schedule of average lag; it does not by itself explain K8's multi-timescale kernel shape or training benefit.

The curvature statistic needs a noise correction

SOAP, Shampoo, and related methods accumulate matrix factors formed from a gradient matrix \(G_t\):

\[ L_t=G_tG_t^{\mathsf T}, \qquad R_t=G_t^{\mathsf T}G_t. \]

A noisy gradient outer product contains a minibatch-dependent covariance term. Write replica \(a\)'s microbatch gradient as \(G_t^{(a)}=M_t+\varepsilon_t^{(a)}\), with \(\mathbb E[\varepsilon_t^{(a)}]=0\). If \(C_{L,t}=\mathbb E[\varepsilon_t^{(a)}\varepsilon_t^{(a)\mathsf T}]\), then averaging \(r\) independent replicas gives

\[ \mathbb E[\bar G_t\bar G_t^{\mathsf T}] =M_tM_t^{\mathsf T}+\frac{1}{r}C_{L,t}. \]

Cross-replica products estimate \(M_tM_t^{\mathsf T}\); within-replica products supply the covariance term. The right factor has the analogous decomposition. This lets us reconstruct both factors for the actual training batch size. Temporal accumulation reduces estimator variance around these expectations; it cannot remove an uncorrected covariance term from them.

The same noise-versus-drift calculation then determines the geometric decay of each factor. Exact eigendecomposition supplies a factored preconditioner, and held-out replica prediction shrinks unreliable spectral corrections toward the identity. Constant factors recover the usual fixed-decay optimizer; a correction unsupported by replica prediction becomes the identity map.

The immediate path

The K8 annealing check comes first because it can reject replica-clocked momentum without a training run. Every analytic choice is fixed before the three K8 values are consulted, including the conversion from the recorded one-million-token halves to the 524-thousand-token training batch.

The factor clock proceeds independently and is the main second-order deliverable. The momentum interaction is tested only after that factored metric predicts held-out replicas better than a fixed-decay estimate. A negligible interaction would let temporal filtering handle gradient history while factored curvature handles within-step geometry. A measurable interaction would identify which temporal degrees of freedom curvature preconditioning makes redundant.

Full-Hessian depth remains a bounded comparison. It earns additional compute only if the factored study leaves a specific discrepancy that deeper curvature could explain.
